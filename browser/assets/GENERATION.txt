Edit this exact 4x4 UI icon sprite atlas. Preserve all sixteen icons, positions,
equal grid and symbols. Remove EVERY bit of the milky white background, purple
fog and diffuse glow: outside the actual sharp icon strokes must be 100%
transparent alpha=0, including all cell corners and between icons. No white or
gray rectangular backgrounds, absolutely no halos. Flatten the icons to clean
very light lavender (#cbb8ed) thin crisp outlines with a tiny violet edge only
inside the stroke, not 3D. This is for use at 24px on an almost black browser:
maximum clarity and no tile backgrounds. Keep row order exactly: back forward
refresh home; star download puzzle profile; gear shield plus close; minimize
maximize search video. Preserve perfectly centered equal 4x4 grid.

Au rendu, les cellules du sprite sont échantillonnées et le masque alpha Qt
supprime les pixels de fond translucides ; aucun fichier source n'est écrasé.
